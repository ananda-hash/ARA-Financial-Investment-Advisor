Investment Philosophy

Process-Driven.
Conviction-Led.

01 — Universe Screening
Quantitative Filter
IDX universe filtered through 12 quantitative screens: ROE, ROIC, debt-to-equity, free cash flow yield, earnings momentum, and Islamic compliance (DES). Reduces 900+ stocks to ~80 candidates.
02 — Deep Fundamental
Business Quality Analysis
Sector-by-sector fundamental analysis covering competitive moat, management track record, earnings quality, and 3-year DCF valuation. Targets 15–25 high-conviction names.
03 — Risk Overlay
Macro & Tail Risk
Portfolio stress-tested against interest rate shifts, commodity cycles, and BI policy changes. Hard drawdown circuit-breakers at portfolio and individual position levels.
04 — Execution
Systematic Entry & Exit
Technical timing overlaid on fundamental thesis for optimal entry. Trailing stop-loss framework with defined profit-taking tranches at 20%, 40%, and 60% gain targets.
05 — Monitor
Continuous Surveillance
Monthly portfolio review against thesis drivers. Earnings revision triggers, corporate action monitoring, and sector rotation signals reviewed weekly by the investment committee.
◆ PRINCIPLE I
Asymmetric Return
We only enter positions where the upside potential exceeds downside risk by at least 3:1 on a risk-adjusted, probability-weighted basis.
◆ PRINCIPLE II
Islamic Finance First
All equity positions comply with OJK Daftar Efek Syariah. We apply the ARA Islamic ESG Filter as an additional screen beyond regulatory minimum.
◆ PRINCIPLE III
Data Over Narrative
Investment decisions are driven by quantifiable evidence — financial ratios, earnings revisions, valuation spreads — not market storytelling or consensus opinion.
◆ PRINCIPLE IV
Transparency of Record
Full audited track record available. We report performance gross and net of fees, with attribution analysis by position and sector quarterly.
